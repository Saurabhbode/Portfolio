The product

operational platform.

SpineGen-i brings users, teams, projects, work allocation, claims, AR aging, initial coding, timesheets, invoices, productivity, reporting, and documents into a single system for operations, finance, management, and administrative teams.

I owned the complete lifecycle: requirements and solution architecture, React frontend development, Spring Boot APIs, database engineering, security, testing, AWS deployment, CI/CD, production support, and continuous enhancement.
